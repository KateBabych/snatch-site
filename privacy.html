Privacy Policy

SNATCH! Draw the Heist · Effective date: November 1, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game SNATCH! Draw the Heist (the “App”), developed by Kateryna Babych, handles your information.

We do not collect personal data

The App does not require an account and does not collect, store or share any personal information. We do not use analytics, tracking or advertising identifiers.

Game progress stays on your device

Your progress (levels, coins, creatures and settings) is saved only locally on your device. It is never sent to us. Deleting the App deletes this data.

Fonts

On first launch the App may download its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any internet request, this technically reveals your device’s IP address to Google; the App sends no other data. See Google’s privacy policy at policies.google.com/privacy.

Children

The App is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect any information from children or anyone else.

Changes

If a future version adds features that handle data (for example, optional rewarded ads), this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about privacy: clientsfancy@gmail.com
