EN

Draw a line. Steal the egg. Don't get caught!

SNATCH! is a sneaky one-finger puzzle game. Plan the route of a little masked thief, slip past guards, cameras and lasers, grab the precious egg and escape. Then hatch it in your secret lair and collect adorable creatures.

  • 112 heists in 11 worlds, plus an endless mode
  • Hold your finger still to wait — perfect timing beats every guard
  • Hatch and raise 112 creatures, decorate your lair, throw parties
  • Play offline, anytime
